Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ASE Studio collects across its products, including Kesher, how it's used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information such as your name and email address; session data such as the video, audio, chat messages, whiteboard content, and poll responses generated while a Kesher session is active; and basic technical data such as device type and app version, used to keep our products working.

2. How we use it

To run sessions — connecting host and attendees, displaying the whiteboard and chat, and running polls and reactions — and to maintain and improve our products, including diagnosing problems and building Kesher's iOS and Android apps.

3. Session data and recording

Video, audio and whiteboard content shared in a Kesher session are used to run that session in real time. We do not sell your session content or personal data.

4. Sharing

We share information with service providers that help run our products, such as video infrastructure providers, only as needed to operate them, and we may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep account and session data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide a product, and delete or anonymize it when it's no longer needed, subject to legal requirements.

6. Your choices

You can review and update your account information, and you can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our products evolve. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.
